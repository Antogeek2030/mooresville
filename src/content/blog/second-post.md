Regular maintenance is the single best way to avoid expensive repairs and keep energy costs under control.

## Recommended Schedule

- **Spring**: Full AC tune-up before the cooling season.
- **Fall**: Furnace or heat-pump inspection before winter.
- **Filters**: Check monthly and replace every 1–3 months.

## What a Tune-Up Includes

Technicians clean coils, check refrigerant levels, test safety controls, inspect electrical connections, lubricate moving parts, and verify proper airflow.

## Benefits for Mooresville Homes

The humid Lake Norman climate means systems work hard most of the year. Preventive care reduces the chance of mid-summer breakdowns and helps equipment last longer.
