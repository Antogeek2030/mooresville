A sudden HVAC failure is stressful. Here are practical steps you can take while waiting for a technician.

## If the AC Stops Cooling

1. Check the thermostat settings and batteries.
2. Make sure the outdoor unit has power and is not blocked.
3. Replace a dirty air filter.
4. Turn the system off if you hear grinding or smell burning.

## If the Heat Stops Working

1. Confirm the thermostat is set to heat and the temperature is raised.
2. Check the furnace switch and circuit breaker.
3. Look at the pilot light or ignition (if accessible and safe).
4. Keep space heaters away from flammable materials.
