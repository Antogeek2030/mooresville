Hot North Carolina summers put heavy demand on air conditioning systems. Catching problems early can save you from a complete failure on the hottest day of the year.

## 1. Weak or Warm Airflow

If the air coming from your vents feels weak or only slightly cool, the system is struggling. Common causes include dirty filters, low refrigerant, or a failing blower motor.

## 2. Unusual Noises

Grinding, squealing, or banging sounds are not normal. These often point to worn bearings, loose parts, or compressor issues that need prompt attention.

## 3. Higher Than Normal Energy Bills

A sudden jump in your electric bill without a change in usage often means the AC is running longer or less efficiently to reach the set temperature.

## 4. Frequent Cycling On and Off

Short cycling wears out components faster and fails to properly dehumidify your home. It can be caused by an oversized unit, thermostat problems, or refrigerant issues.

## 5. Musty or Burning Odors

Musty smells may indicate mold in the system or drain pan. A burning smell can signal electrical problems — turn the system off and call for service immediately.
